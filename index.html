Sal Pace

Postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Advanced Study (IAS) at Princeton

I’m a theoretical physicist studying the laws of quantum physics. My research focuses on uncovering the organizing principles underlying highly entangled, strongly interacting quantum systems and exploring their implications for condensed matter, quantum information, and high-energy physics. My current work uses symmetry principles to clarify the relationship between quantum field theories and quantum lattice systems, and to classify, constrain, and characterize patterns of quantum entanglement and phases of quantum matter.

I received my PhD in physics from MIT in 2026 under the supervision of Xiao-Gang Wen. Previously, I earned an MPhil in physics from the University of Cambridge in 2021 as a Churchill Scholar and a BA/MA in physics from Boston University in 2020.
